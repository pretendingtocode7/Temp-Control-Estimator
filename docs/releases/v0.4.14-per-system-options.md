# v0.4.14 per-system alternatives and proposal options

Category 2 filters the exact provider values: Standard-Efficiency SILVER,
High-Efficiency SILVER, Standard-Efficiency GOLD, High-Efficiency GOLD,
Standard-Efficiency PLATINUM and High-Efficiency PLATINUM. Structured Category 2
values combine with category, search, input BTU, tonnage and AFUE before pagination.
Names or Package Tier tags do not substitute for a missing/conflicting custom
field. Items without Category 2 remain available under All. The actual Package
reporting-tag eligibility rule remains unchanged.

## Physical systems and alternative offers

Add another system means an additional physical installation. Add alternative
creates a comparison offer inside one physical system. It copies selected
equipment, accessories and notes into a new stable choice identity, with an
editable label and customer sales price. It does not automatically choose the
new alternative. Existing equipment is labeled from its actual Category 2 when
available, otherwise Current selection; it is never silently labeled Silver.

Each physical system has one selected choice for pricing. An editing choice is
separate from that pricing selection. Only chosen equipment contributes to
equipment cost, catalog sales subtotal, Books lines and CRM equipment rows. All
offered alternatives are freshly validated, including unselected offers.

For example, two systems with option prices 8/10/12 thousand and 9/11/13 thousand
start at 17 thousand when both lowest-priced options are selected. Choosing the
first system's middle option gives 19 thousand; choosing the second system's top
option then gives 23 thousand. All six alternatives are never charged as 63
thousand. Each option has an independently editable installed sales price.

Common project charges or discounts apply once through project_adjustment. A
prior manually entered total is migrated into that shared adjustment when
alternatives are introduced. Later choice changes recompute the project total
with that same adjustment. The UI's purchase cost remains internal; the legacy
wire subtotal retains selected catalog sales semantics for Books compatibility.

The versioned choice payload carries stable choice_id, label, equipment identity
and description overrides, sales_price and selected_choice_id. Client-selected
equipment mirrors and internal proposal metadata are discarded in favor of the
validated selected choice. Bounds cover ten physical systems, six choices each,
two hundred unique referenced catalog items and finite bounded prices. Existing
package/component/duplicate rules and successful idempotency replays remain.

## Optional zero-price items

Both options are unchecked by default and apply once per proposal at zero today.
The authenticated addon endpoint separately resolves the unique active exact-name
Books item and checks its fresh identity, noncomposite flag and zero sales rate.
It never grants these untagged items access to the equipment picker. Missing,
ambiguous, changed or unavailable items cannot be newly selected; a previously
checked unavailable item can still be explicitly cleared. Generation revalidates
the selected options rather than trusting cached availability or client IDs.

New Residential System Maintenance Agreement is a zero-price Books line. Its
customer wording is: Renews at $349 for one unit. The future renewal is not part
of today's total, and additional-unit renewal pricing is not invented.

Permits & Inspections is a zero-price Books line with these exact paragraphs:

> Permit fees are not included in the project total.
>
> Temp Control will prepare and complete all required permit paperwork and submit the necessary documentation to the local township on the homeowner’s behalf.
>
> All work will be performed in accordance with local building codes and manufacturer specifications. Permit payment, pickup and scheduling of Inspections are the responsibility of the homeowner.

An existing permit equipment line conflicts with the new zero-price paperwork
option and must be removed explicitly before selecting it. It is never silently
dropped or charged while the proposal says permit fees are excluded. Legacy paid
permit overrides remain supported when the new option is unchecked.

## Documents and customer selections

The app-generated proposal groups all alternatives under their physical system,
shows each option's customer sales price, and marks which choice contributes to
the project total. Component unit prices, purchase costs and markup remain hidden.
Selected-only Books lines and CRM rows stay separate from this comparison view.
Zero-price add-ons are not CRM equipment rows. Exact selected policies appear in
the PDF and default Books descriptions, terms, annexure and email content.

The homeowner selection page offers one choice per physical system. An explicit
Update my estimate action updates the existing Books estimate using the quoted
option prices and shared adjustment; opening a link or choosing a radio button
does not write to Books. The homeowner can review and approve the final estimate
in Books after the selected configuration has synchronized. Choosing equipment
does not itself approve an estimate, create a transaction or send another email.
The previously prepared comparison PDF remains a snapshot of the offered options
and its initial selection. A later customer choice updates Books and the selected
CRM equipment; it does not regenerate that earlier attachment. The final Books
estimate is the configuration to review and approve after a change.

Staff can create or revoke a scoped selection link after generation. The
existing explicit Send flow includes that link for proposals with alternatives;
legacy proposals keep the existing review link. Queued email jobs retain the
original authorized staff owner. A failed link check stops that email rather
than substituting an approval link that bypasses package selection.

The public page has a generic, uncached shell without a staff nonce or
service-worker registration. It renders at early init, before ordinary template
and enqueue hooks. Its CSP allows only the exact verified local bundle, with
SHA-384 integrity, and denies inline handlers, frames and workers. Missing or
unsafe bundle identity fails closed. No global hosting, plugin hooks or output
buffers are changed. The no-transform header is advisory; downstream injection
and browser enforcement must still be checked after installation. Its expiring bearer stays in the URL
fragment and JSON request body. Public responses contain offered equipment
descriptions and customer sales prices, never internal purchase costs or markup.
The server binds the estimate to the original successful generation audit and
independently resolved customer, validates offered selections, and rejects
revoked links, stale revisions, expired/final estimates and independent changes.
Only the bound existing Books estimate may be updated. Quoted prices remain
frozen, while current catalog eligibility and zero-price addons are rechecked.

Pending provider operations are recorded before an update. Repeating the same
request checks its existing result rather than blindly repeating a Books write.
CRM equipment synchronization preserves the selected-only projection and must
finish before the final Books review link is shown. Provider writes and email
delivery are tested with synthetic in-memory doubles, never real customer
records. Full WordPress/provider integration remains a separate verification
boundary; development tests do not demonstrate real email delivery or approval.

CRM subform updates use the existing owned row identities and delete only owned
slots removed by a selection. Independent rows remain outside this update.
Unknown outcomes are reconciled by reads before further writes. This follows
[Zoho's subform update contract](https://www.zoho.com/crm/developer/docs/api/v8/update-subforms.html):
omitting a row identity appends a row, so a blind retry could duplicate equipment.

The customer update path currently supports verified USD estimates with unit
quantities, no line tax, discount or shipping, and unchanged customer/financial
context. A quote outside that supported context requires office review. The CRM
deal must still belong to the audited account and estimate and contain the
expected owned equipment rows. Missing fields, permissions or changed scope
fail closed rather than updating another record or claiming synchronization.

New and reused selection links use the dedicated customer page under the existing
REST namespace and include the nonsecret release version in the query string,
with the bearer still confined to the fragment. When the page is inactive or its
selector flag is absent, that address returns inert JSON instead of falling back
to a cached marketing page. Its configured URL must remain HTTPS and on the home
origin before any customer/provider lookup or selection-record creation. The staff URL
validator permits only that optional single bounded semantic version alongside
the selector flag. This avoids older browser/server responses cached for the
unversioned home-page query. Public JS and CSS URLs additionally carry independent
content fingerprints; the exact-path CSP and full SHA-384 script integrity remain
required. Asset verification failures, including CSS, fail closed.

A narrowly scoped deployment hold prevents issuing or using customer choice links
while public delivery is being verified. It does not change existing quote data
or legacy proposals without alternatives. Invalid tokens retain their generic
unauthorized response. The operator removes only this release-owned hold after
actual served-asset, CSP and isolated-browser checks pass; it is not a replacement
for the normal authorization and provider guards.

Read-only inspection of an existing audited live deal confirmed the account and
Books bindings, but its equipment subform exposes only the core identity fields
and nullable Brand/Model. The customer flow freezes the supported known-field
contract from those actual rows. Required slot, system, item and row identities
remain exact; optional empty legacy descriptors retain an unknown baseline.
Subsequent additions or removals of known fields require office review. Only
supported fields are updated and read back, while Books holds the selected scope
and price. No CRM schema or credential change is needed.

Stored template posts and the exact original v0.4.8 installer/reference files
remain unchanged. Release packaging projects explicit PHP overrides and four
explicit new proposal/customer-choice files into a separate versioned installable.
Build, synthetic integration, desktop/mobile and PDF evidence are recorded by
the release operator; live deployment status is not inferred from this source.

## Offline verification

The frozen candidate passed 277 frontend unit tests and 16 desktop/mobile browser
cases, including ten homeowner cases. The production build preserves all 45
original reference files and the exact installer. Both generated comparison PDFs
have two complete Letter pages; every page and the mobile customer controls were
visually reviewed.

Focused PHP checks passed: catalog 97 cases/1,004 assertions; proposal projection
46/200; document/generator 24/502; customer choice service 176/958; actual Zoho
dispatcher with mocked HTTP 10/32; queued email integration 14/36; public shell
41 checks across 31 isolated PHP cases using the actual rebuilt assets. The combined
projected runtime passed 71 existing smoke assertions and 33 PHPUnit tests/161
assertions, with seven expected local WordPress/libsodium skips. All 51 projected
production/test PHP files parsed under PHP 8.1.34. An independent read-only
security review reran the customer service and actual dispatcher checks.

These checks use synthetic customer/provider records and forbid real email,
Books/CRM writes or off-site browser requests. Native Books PDF styling, actual
CRM custom-field permissions, WordPress multiworker behavior, physical Safari/iOS
and real approval/payment remain untested. Packaging records the final pinned
commit, build hashes and installable manifest separately from this source.
